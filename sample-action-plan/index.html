Client
O*** Distributors (Illustrative Case)

8-Week Implementation Plan

Confidentiality: Sample case. All data and findings are fictional.

Objective

Improve cash-flow visibility, restore profitability, optimise working capital and strengthen financial decision-making through clear actions, ownership and deadlines.

PriorityActionOwnerDeadlineSuccess measure
1 · Cash FlowDevelop a rolling 13-week cash-flow forecast covering collections, supplier payments, operating expenses and debt commitments.Finance ManagerWeek 2Forecast updated weekly with cash risks identified in advance.
2 · ProfitabilityAnalyse gross margins by product, supplier and customer; investigate the decline from 25% to 23%.Finance Manager + Sales ManagerWeek 3Margin-loss analysis completed and corrective actions agreed.
3 · ReceivablesReview overdue customer balances, assign collection responsibilities and implement a weekly follow-up process.Credit ControllerWeek 2Reduce receivables days from 64 to 55 or fewer.
PriorityActionOwnerDeadlineSuccess measure
4 · InventoryIdentify slow-moving stock, review purchasing patterns and improve replenishment decisions.Inventory Manager + Procurement ManagerWeek 4Reduce inventory days from 87 to 75 or fewer without disrupting service.
5 · Cost ControlClassify major costs, investigate budget variances and identify opportunities to reduce avoidable expenditure.Finance Manager + Department HeadsWeek 5Monthly cost-variance report with corrective actions and owners.
