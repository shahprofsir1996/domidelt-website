1. Summary of Findings

O*** Distributors is a medium-sized wholesale distribution business with a growing sales base. The financial diagnostic shows that while revenue increased, profitability and cash generation have weakened. The business has a solid foundation in terms of basic financial reporting, but there are significant opportunities to improve cost control, working capital management and forward-looking financial planning.

2. Strengths

O*** Distributors has a solid foundation in several areas:

  1. Established customer base: Recurring sales and long-term client relationships.
  2. Solid revenue growth: Sales increased by 17.6% year-on-year.
  3. Basic financial reporting: Monthly management accounts are available.
  4. Positive cash balance: Adequate cash reserves at year-end.
  5. Clear management team: Experienced leadership with operational knowledge.
  6. Budget in place: Annual budget exists and is being used for planning.

3. Gaps and Risks

Several areas need attention to reduce risk and improve performance:

  1. Margin pressure: Gross profit margin declined from 25% to 23%, and net profit margin fell to 1.9%.
  2. Weak liquidity: Quick ratio of 0.73x indicates reliance on inventory and receivables.
  3. Slow customer collections: Receivables days at 64 (target ≤55).
  1. High inventory levels: Inventory days at 87 (target ≤75).
  2. Limited product-level insight: No detailed profitability analysis by product or customer.
  3. Incomplete forecasting: No rolling cash forecast or scenario planning.
