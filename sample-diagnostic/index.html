1. Executive Summary

O*** Distributors is a fictional medium-sized wholesale distribution business supplying consumer and business products to retailers and commercial customers. The company has experienced sales growth, but its financial review indicates that growth has not translated into a proportionate improvement in profitability or cash generation.

The sample diagnostic identifies three principal areas requiring management attention:

  • Declining profitability: Revenue has increased, but gross profit margins have weakened, while operating costs remain significant.
  • Working capital pressure: A substantial amount of cash is tied up in inventory and customer receivables.
  • Inadequate forward-looking reporting: Management has periodic financial statements and a basic budget, but lacks a reliable short-term cash forecast and sufficiently detailed product-level profitability reporting.

The business is not assumed to be failing. It is generating accounting profit and growing sales. The concern is whether it is managing that growth efficiently and converting it into sustainable cash flow.

2. Business Profile and Scope

2.1 Business background

O*** Distributors operates as a wholesale distributor. It purchases products from suppliers, holds inventory and sells to business customers, often on credit.

Its financial performance therefore depends on more than sales volume. Management must control purchase prices, selling prices, inventory levels, customer credit, supplier payment terms, operating expenses and financing costs.
