Project 360

Strategy, Innovation & SME Growth

See the whole business. Challenge the status quo. Plan the next stage of growth.

Project 360 helps business owners step back from day-to-day operations, evaluate their business as a whole and identify what needs to change to achieve sustainable growth. From strategic planning and business model development to innovation, market expansion and performance improvement, we help turn business challenges into clear priorities and actionable plans.
